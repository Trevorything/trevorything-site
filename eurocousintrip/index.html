⚠️ Critical Excursion Warnings

🏰 Neuschwanstein Castle

Tickets drop exactly 3 months in advance and sell out rapidly. Make sure to book online ahead of time. Don't skip Marienbrucke bridge for the absolute best view of the castle.

❄️ Eisriesenwelt Ice Caves

Warm clothes and sturdy shoes are mandatory! Even in early October, the cave interior is below freezing (32°F / 0°C). The tour requires walking up 1,400 stairs.
